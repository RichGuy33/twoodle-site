Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

One of you creates a pair in Twoodle and shares the invite code. The other enters it. Pairing is part of Twoodle Pro, and one subscription covers both of you.

My widget isn't updating.

Twoodle refreshes the widget when your partner sends something, but iOS decides exactly when a widget redraws, so there can be a short delay. Open Twoodle once and check that notifications are allowed. If it still doesn't update, email us.

How do I delete a doodle?

In the Gallery, touch and hold the doodle and choose Delete doodle. This removes the image and its record for both of you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting your Twoodle account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I turn off distance sharing?

Turn the distance feature off in Twoodle. That erases your stored position. You can also revoke location access for Twoodle in iOS Settings at any time.

How do I delete my account?

In Twoodle, go to Settings and choose Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your sign-in, profile, location, pair and everything shared in it, for both of you, so your partner's pair ends too. If you can't open the app, email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Is there an Android version?

Not right now. Twoodle is iPhone only and requires iOS 18 or later.
