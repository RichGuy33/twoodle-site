Privacy summary

Based on the privacy policy effective November 6, 2026

This is a short summary. The full privacy policy is the authoritative version: read the full Twoodle privacy policy.

The short version

  • Twoodle has no ads, doesn't sell your data, and doesn't track you across other apps.
  • What you share goes to your one paired partner.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app, and it removes your data for both of you.

What Twoodle collects

  • Account: no sign-up is needed. The app creates an account with a random user ID. If you save it with Sign in with Apple or email, we store that email.
  • Profile (optional): a display name and profile photo, shown to your partner.
  • What you share: doodles, photos placed on a canvas, notes, moods, nudges, important dates and widget choices, so your partner's app and widgets can show them.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on the distance widget and allow access. Only your latest position is kept, and turning the feature off erases it.
  • Purchases: subscriptions go through the App Store. RevenueCat receives purchase history to unlock Pro. We never see your card details.
  • Anonymous analytics: a small, fixed set of setup and subscription events, sent to PostHog without your name, email, content or location.

Who processes it

Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), Apple (sign-in, notifications, payments), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and PostHog (anonymous analytics).

Your choices

  • Delete individual doodles, notes and your profile photo in the app.
  • Turn off location sharing at any time.
  • Delete your whole account under Settings, then Delete my account.
  • Questions or a data request? Email twoodlesupport@gmail.com.
