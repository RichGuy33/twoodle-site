Couple Joy Alternative: A Simpler Way to Stay Close on iPhone

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, so we're biased. We've kept to facts from each app's App Store page and said plainly where Couple Joy is the better pick.

Some couples apps are a Swiss Army knife. Some are a single very good spoon.

Couple Joy is closer to the knife. If you open it and think, "I only wanted to send my person a little something," this guide is for you.

Short answer: if you want a lighter app that puts doodles and notes on your partner's iPhone widgets, Twoodle is a reasonable alternative. If you want daily questions, quizzes and a shared journal, stay with Couple Joy. Details below.

What Couple Joy actually offers

Per its App Store listing, Couple Joy (by HEARTBIT S.R.L., subtitled "Long Distance & Love Tracker") is a free download with in-app purchases. Its description highlights couple widgets, daily questions and quizzes with chat, a shared photo journal, countdowns to birthdays and anniversaries, mood sharing, distance tracking, games and a daily streak. It runs on iPhone, and its listing shows about a 4.7 rating from roughly 4.3k ratings.

That's a lot of app, and plenty of couples love that. Features change, so check the listing for the current list.

Why people look for something else

  • They want one simple habit (send a doodle) rather than a feature buffet.
  • They'd rather the app lived on their Home Screen than ask for daily check-ins.
  • They want to know exactly what they're paying for up front.

The lighter option: doodles on a Home Screen widget

Twoodle is an iPhone app for couples. You draw on a blank canvas or a photo, send it, and it shows up on your partner's Home Screen widget. Lock Screen widgets cover love notes and a days-together counter, and there's an optional distance widget. You can also share moods, send nudges and draw together live on a shared canvas inside the app.

One honest detail: doodles land on the Home Screen widget, not the Lock Screen. The Lock Screen is for notes and your day counter. We explain the whole setup in how to draw on your partner's iPhone Lock Screen and widgets.

When Couple Joy is the better pick

  • You want conversation prompts. Daily questions and quizzes are a real strength, and Twoodle has none.
  • You want a shared journal and countdowns in one place.
  • One of you has an older iPhone. Couple Joy lists iOS 16.4 as the minimum; Twoodle needs iOS 18.

When Twoodle is the better pick

  • You mostly want to send small, silly drawings that appear on a Home Screen.
  • You like the Lock Screen day counter and love notes without extra features around them.
  • You both have iOS 18 or later and want one subscription for two people.
