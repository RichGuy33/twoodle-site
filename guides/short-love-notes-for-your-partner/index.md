# Short Love Notes for Your Partner: 30 Ideas That Fit on a Lock Screen

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

> **Disclosure:** we make Twoodle, an iPhone app for couples. These notes work in any app, or on a sticky note, and we say where Twoodle fits.

Short answer: a good short love note is one line, names one specific thing, and needs no reply. Thirty you can steal are below.

Confession: most of us freeze when we try to write something sweet. We open the text box, type "I love you," delete it because it feels too small, type three paragraphs, delete those because it feels like a hostage letter, and send "hey :)".

The fix is smaller, not bigger. Here's the formula, then the list.

## The 20-second formula

Pick one of these three and finish the sentence:

- **Noticed:** "I noticed you..." (hummed while cooking, kept my side of the bed warm, said hi to the dog first).
- **Thankful:** "Thank you for..." (the small thing, not the big thing).
- **Looking forward:** "I can't wait to..." (a specific plan, even a dumb one).

Specific beats poetic every time. "You make my life better" is nice. "You make Tuesdays survivable" is a note someone screenshots.

## 30 short love notes you can steal

### Sweet and simple

1. Thinking of you. That's the whole note.
2. You're my favorite notification.
3. Today is better because you exist in it.
4. I picked you again this morning.
5. Still the best part of my day.
6. Just checking: yes, I still like you a lot.
7. Whatever happens today, I'm on your team.
8. You're my favorite person to be quiet with.

### Funny

1. I'd share my fries with you. That's real love.
2. You're the reason I check my phone, and not for the news.
3. Roses are red, my brain is fried, I still want you on my side.
4. Reminder: you are a snack and also a person I deeply respect.
5. I like you more than the last bite of dessert. Please understand the scale of this.
6. Your hugs are my favorite software update.

### For a hard day

1. You don't have to be okay today. I'm here either way.
2. You're doing better than you think.
3. Tonight I'm handling dinner and your complaints. Both.
4. Whatever it is, we'll figure it out together.

### Long-distance

1. Same moon, same me missing you.
2. Counting down to the next time I get to see your face in person.
3. Different time zone, same favorite person.
4. I saved you the better half of my day. Call me when you can.
5. Distance is temporary. This is not.

### Pure appreciation

1. Thank you for being patient with me.
2. I love how you laugh at your own jokes before the punchline.
3. You make ordinary days feel like they count.
4. I'm lucky. Just wanted you to know I know it.
5. Home is wherever you are. Even in line at the grocery store.
6. I'd pick you in every timeline.

Fair warning: if you copy one word for word, add one real detail. "You're my favorite person" plus "even when you steal the blanket" is the difference between a card and a note.
