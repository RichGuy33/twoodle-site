Lovebox Alternative With No Hardware: Doodles on Your Partner's iPhone

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, so we're biased. We've tried to stick to facts and to say where Lovebox is the better choice.

What Lovebox is

Lovebox is a small wooden box with a screen and a heart on the side. You send messages, photos, drawings or stickers from the free Lovebox app, and the heart spins when something arrives. The person who owns the box opens the lid to read it. The box is a physical product you buy, and the app connects to it over Wi-Fi.

Why people look for an alternative

  • They don't want to buy and ship a device.
  • They'd rather see messages on the phone they already check all day.
  • They want something that works the same day, with no setup beyond an app.

The no-hardware option: a widget on your partner's iPhone

Twoodle skips the box. You draw a doodle on a photo or blank canvas, hit send, and it fades onto your partner's Home Screen widget. You can also send short notes to their Lock Screen or Home Screen, share a mood, count your days together, and draw on a shared canvas at the same time.

When Lovebox is the better pick

  • You want a physical object on a desk or nightstand, or a gift to unwrap.
  • Your partner is on Android. Twoodle doesn't have an Android app right now.
  • You like the ritual of opening a box rather than glancing at a phone.

When Twoodle is the better pick

  • You both have iPhones and want nothing extra to buy or ship.
  • You want drawings to show up on the screen you already look at.
  • You like the idea of drawing together live, or of a Lock Screen counter for your days together.
