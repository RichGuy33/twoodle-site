# Long-Distance Relationship Gift Ideas: 25 Thoughtful Gifts You Can Send

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

> **Disclosure:** we make Twoodle, an iPhone app for couples. Almost every idea below needs no app, and we say where Twoodle fits.

Short answer: the best long-distance gift is specific, not expensive. Pick something that lands in their physical world, proves you were paying attention, and gives you both something to talk about on the next call.

Confession: the worst gift in the history of long-distance relationships is the generic one you ordered at 11 p.m. with a gift-wrap upsell. Your partner can tell. Fancy packaging cannot disguise "I panicked."

Here are 25 ideas in five groups, sorted by what they feel like when they arrive.

## Before you buy: three quick checks

- **Delivery time.** Count the days, then add a buffer. A birthday gift that arrives a week late is a story, not a surprise.
- **Borders.** Different countries mean customs, duties and restrictions. Check the postal service's rules for what you can send, and skip food and liquids unless you have confirmed they are allowed.
- **Their space.** Small and flat is easy to keep. A giant stuffed animal is adorable until it needs a seat of its own.

## Paper gifts: cheap, personal, hard to get wrong

- **A handwritten letter.** Boring? No. Almost nobody gets one anymore, which is the point. Need words? Borrow from [30 short love notes](https://twoodle.app/guides/short-love-notes-for-your-partner/).
- **Open-when letters.** A few sealed envelopes: open when you miss me, open when you had a rough day, open when you can't sleep.
- **A photo strip or small print set.** Pick pictures of the two of you and one of something only you two would understand.
- **A hand-drawn map.** Where you met, where you'll live someday, where the first date went wrong.
- **A coupon book of video-call dates.** Cooking together, a movie night, a walk-and-talk. Ideas: [20 long-distance date ideas](https://twoodle.app/guides/long-distance-date-ideas/).

## Care-package gifts: a small box of you

- **Their favorite snack, from your side of the world** (after the customs check above).
- **A shirt or hoodie that smells like you.** Slightly unglamorous, deeply effective.
- **A matching pair.** Mugs, keychains or socks. They're only a gift if you each actually use yours.
- **A mystery box with dated tabs.** Small wrapped items numbered for the days until you next meet.
- **A playlist with a note about every song,** printed on a card.

## Shared-experience gifts: something you do together

- **A movie or show night, pre-planned.** Pick the film, send the snacks, press play together.
- **A two-player game.** Whatever you both can run on your devices; test it before gifting it.
- **The same book, two copies.** Read a chapter a week and discuss it on your weekly call.
- **The same recipe and a matching ingredient kit.** Cook it on a call and rate each other's results.
- **A ticket to your next visit.** The ultimate long-distance gift is a date on the calendar. It also makes the wait feel finite; a [Lock Screen counter](https://twoodle.app/guides/days-together-lock-screen-counter/) helps.

## Small daily gifts: the ones that cost nothing

A big parcel is an event. A small daily gesture is a habit, and habits are what long-distance relationships actually run on. These need no postage.

- **A voice note** from your commute or your kitchen.
- **A doodle on their Home Screen.** If you're both on iPhone, Twoodle puts it on your partner's Home Screen widget, where it's waiting next time they unlock. Not sure what to draw? Try [25 things to doodle](https://twoodle.app/guides/things-to-doodle-for-your-partner/), no talent required.
- **A Love Note widget message.** A line they'll see without opening anything.
- **A photo of your ordinary day,** meant to say "you're in my day too."
- **A good-night message timed for their morning.** See [the time zones guide](https://twoodle.app/guides/long-distance-relationship-time-zones/) for how to keep your clocks fair.

## How to choose between them

Match the gift to the moment. Anniversaries and birthdays deserve a parcel. Rough weeks deserve something small and immediate. The routine in between is covered in [how to stay connected without texting 24/7](https://twoodle.app/guides/how-to-stay-connected-in-a-long-distance-relationship/).

And one rule beats all the rest: add a note saying why you chose it. "I saw this and thought of the night we got lost in the rain" turns a mug into a memory.

## FAQ

### What is a good gift for a long-distance relationship?

Something that arrives in their physical world and reminds them of you: a handwritten letter, a care package built around an inside joke, a shared-experience gift you can use together over a video call, or a small object they will see daily. Specific beats expensive.

### What can I send my long-distance partner that is cheap?

A handwritten letter, a printed photo strip, a playlist with a note about each song, a coupon book of video-call dates, or a small box of their favorite snacks. Postage is often the biggest cost, so keep the parcel flat and light.

### What if we are in different countries?

Check customs rules and delivery times before you buy, and avoid food or liquids unless you have confirmed they can be sent. Flat paper gifts and digital gifts are the lowest-risk options.
