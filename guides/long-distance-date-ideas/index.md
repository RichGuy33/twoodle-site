# Long-Distance Date Ideas: 20 Dates That Work Across Time Zones

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

> **Disclosure:** we make Twoodle, an iPhone app for couples. Almost every idea below needs no app at all, and we say where Twoodle fits.

Short answer: the best long-distance date is one you can actually start. Pick something tiny, put a time on it, and do the same thing at the same time, even from two different continents.

Picture this: it's Sunday, you've been "meaning to plan something" for three weeks, and the plan has become a very large, very cancelled thing. Now picture a 20-minute date with a snack. That one happens.

Here are 20 ideas, grouped by how much energy you've got. Pick one for this week.

## Video-call dates (you have an hour)

1. **Cook the same recipe.** Agree on a dish, cook with the call propped on the counter, then eat together. Burnt bits are part of the date.
2. **Watch something together.** FaceTime SharePlay works in supported apps, and some streaming services have their own watch-party tools. Test it before date night, not during.
3. **Play a game.** Pick a free online game, a trivia night or a shared-screen board game. Keep the stakes low and the trash talk high.
4. **Do a virtual tour.** Many museums and zoos share online tours. Wander around somewhere neither of you has been.
5. **Dress up for dinner.** Table, candles, nice shirt, bad lighting. The fancier the setup, the funnier it gets.
6. **Plan a trip.** Open a map and pick the place you'll meet next. Fantasy itineraries count.

## Low-effort dates (you have a tired evening)

1. **Leave the call open.** Do chores, read, fold laundry, together but separately. This is underrated.
2. **Share a playlist.** Add five songs each and listen on the same night. Guess whose song is whose.
3. **Read the same book.** Read a chapter a week and talk about it on Sunday.
4. **Order each other dinner.** If delivery works in both cities, pick something for the other person. Dessert counts.
5. **Go on a walk and talk.** Take the call outside. Describe what you see so the other person gets the neighborhood.
6. **Do a photo swap.** Each of you sends three photos from this week and explains them.

## Tiny dates (you have two minutes)

1. **Send a doodle.** Draw anything: your lunch, your face, a tiny dog. In Twoodle it shows up on your partner's Home Screen widget.
2. **Send a love note.** One sentence is enough. Twoodle's Love Note widget can sit on the Lock Screen or Home Screen.
3. **Answer one question each.** Pick a prompt (best thing today, worst thing today) and trade answers.
4. **Check the clocks.** Add their city to your World Clock and send something small at their lunch break.

## Dates that mark something

1. **A countdown date.** (More in our [countdown guide](https://twoodle.app/guides/long-distance-countdown-until-we-meet/).) On the weekend before you meet, make a list of everything you'll do. The [Days Together widget](https://twoodle.app/guides/days-together-lock-screen-counter/) can keep the count on your Lock Screen.
2. **An anniversary date.** Recreate your first date as closely as possible, from two kitchens.
3. **A time-capsule date.** Write each other a letter to open in six months.
4. **A surprise delivery.** Send flowers, a snack or a handwritten card so it arrives during the call.

## FAQ

### What are good long-distance date ideas?

Start with simple ones: a video call while you cook the same recipe, a shared playlist, watching a movie together with FaceTime SharePlay or a streaming watch party, or a stay-on-the-call chore night. Pick one that fits both time zones.

### How often should long-distance couples have a date?

There's no rule. Many couples find one planned date a week plus small daily check-ins works, but the right rhythm is whatever you will both actually keep.

### What can we do on a date when we only have 10 minutes?

Send each other a quick doodle or love note, swap one photo of where you are, or do a two-minute walk and talk. Small counts.
