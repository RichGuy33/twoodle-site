How to Add a Couples Widget to Your iPhone Home Screen (Step by Step)

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, an iPhone app for couples. The steps below are standard iOS, so they work for any couples widget app. We use Twoodle for the examples.

Short version: touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap +, search for your app, pick a size, tap Add Widget. Then make your partner do the same on their phone. Two minutes, tops.

That last part is where most couples get stuck. One of you installs the app, sends a lovely little doodle, and the other one says "I don't see anything." Because they never put the widget on their screen. The app is the sender. The widget is the mailbox.

Here's the full walkthrough, plus what to do when the widget goes missing or stops updating.

Add a widget to your Home Screen

  1. Install the app and open it once. (Widgets show up in the picker more reliably after the app has been opened.)
  2. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons start to wiggle.
  3. Tap the + button in the top corner.
  4. Search for the app by name. For us, that's Twoodle.
  5. Swipe through the available widgets and sizes, then tap Add Widget.
  6. Drag it where you want it and tap Done.

You can also tap Edit in the top corner while the icons wiggle, then Add Widget. Same result.

Which size should you pick?

Opinionated take: go bigger than feels reasonable. A doodle in a tiny widget is a smudge. A small widget works for a love note or a counter; a bigger one is better for a drawing you want to actually see. Check the picker in the app you use to see which sizes it offers.

You can also drop a widget on top of another to make a stack and swipe between them, which is handy if you want your partner's widget and, say, your calendar in one spot.

Add a widget to your Lock Screen

  1. Wake your iPhone and touch and hold the Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize, then choose Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area under the clock and pick a widget.
  4. Tap Done.

Widget missing, blank or stale?

  • Not in the widget list. Open the app once, then search again. Still nothing? Restart the iPhone.
  • Showing old content. iOS decides when widgets redraw, so there can be a short delay after your partner sends something. Open the app, and check that notifications are allowed.
  • Nothing arrives. Check that both of you are paired and that your partner actually added the widget.
  • Wrong widget type. Doodles go to the Home Screen widget, not the Lock Screen.
  • Want to remove one. Touch and hold the widget, tap Remove Widget, then confirm.

If it still misbehaves, contact support. A real person (a small team, one very opinionated founder) will read it.

FAQ

How do I add a couples widget to my iPhone Home Screen?

Install the couples app, then touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for the app, pick a widget and size, and tap Add Widget. Your partner has to do the same on their phone.

Why can't I find the widget in the list?

Open the app once after installing it, make sure it's installed on that iPhone, then try the search again. If it still isn't there, restart the iPhone and retry.

Can I put a couples widget on my Lock Screen?

Some of them. With Twoodle, the Love Note widget can go on the Lock Screen or Home Screen and the Days Together widget goes on the Lock Screen. Doodles appear on the Home Screen widget only.

Does my partner need to add the widget too?

Yes. The widget on their phone is what displays what you send, so each of you adds your own.
