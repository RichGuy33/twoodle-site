Widgetable Alternative for Couples: Simpler, No Pets or Ads

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, so we're biased. Everything about Widgetable below comes from its App Store listing, and we say plainly where it's the better pick.

Imagine you open an app to send your partner a quick "thinking of you."

Twenty seconds later you're feeding a virtual shrimp to a pet you now co-parent. Nobody planned this.

Short answer: Widgetable is a big, playful widget app built mostly around friends and couples sharing small updates. If you want something narrower, where the whole point is a doodle landing on your partner's Home Screen, Twoodle is a reasonable alternative. If you want pets, sleep tracking and a mountain of customization, stay with Widgetable.

What Widgetable actually offers

Per its App Store listing, "Widgetable: Besties & Couples" (subtitle "Share Whatever on BFF's Screen") is a free download with in-app purchases, for iPhone only, requiring iOS 16.0 or later. The listing shows a 4.9 rating from about 389K ratings.

Its description covers Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, co-parented virtual pets, a sleep widget, mood widgets, a distance widget, a "Miss You" counter, plant widgets, themed wallpapers, and a "Pin It!" feature for sending snaps, emojis, doodles and text to a friend's screen. The listing also notes loot boxes and advertising, and lists in-app purchases such as a Premium subscription (an annual option at $19.99 with a free trial, and monthly options) plus diamonds, pet eggs and pet food. Prices vary, so check the listing for your region.

That's a lot of app, and it's clearly beloved. It's just not a small one.

Why couples look for something else

  • The app is built for "besties and loved ones" in general, and they want something made for two.
  • They'd rather not have ads or a virtual-currency shop between them and a simple message.
  • They want drawing to be the main event, not one feature among dozens.

The narrower option: doodles on a Home Screen widget

Twoodle is an iPhone app for couples. You draw on a blank canvas or a photo, send it, and it appears on your partner's Home Screen widget. Lock Screen widgets cover love notes and a days-together counter, and there's an optional distance widget. You can also share moods, send nudges and draw together live on a shared canvas inside the app.

One honest detail: doodles land on the Home Screen widget, not the Lock Screen. The Lock Screen is for notes and your day counter. Our guide on drawing on your partner's iPhone widgets walks through the setup.

On privacy: no ads, no selling your data, no cross-app tracking. Location is used only if you turn on the Distance widget.
