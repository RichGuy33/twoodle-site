Days Together Counter on Your iPhone Lock Screen: How to Set One Up

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, and it has a Days Together widget. We've kept this guide to how iOS works and what to look for, so it's useful whichever app you pick.

Ask someone how long they've been with their partner and you'll get "um, three years? Three and a half?" followed by a quiet panic.

A days-together counter fixes that. It's a number on your Lock Screen that goes up by one every day, and it's weirdly lovely to glance at.

Here's the short answer, then the details: on iOS 16 or later, you add a counter as a Lock Screen widget from an app that provides one. Steps are right below.

How to add a days-together counter to your Lock Screen

  1. Install an app that offers a Lock Screen counter widget (we cover what to look for below).
  2. Wake your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen until the Customize button appears.
  3. Tap Customize, then choose Lock Screen.
  4. Tap the widget area under the time and pick the counter widget from the list.
  5. Tap Done. The count shows up every time you wake your phone.

Lock Screen widgets have limited space, so expect a compact number rather than a big poster. That's a feature: it's quiet, and nobody beside you gets a full anniversary announcement.

What to look for in a relationship day counter

  • A real Lock Screen widget. Plenty of counter apps only offer Home Screen widgets. That works, but it's a screen you see less often than the one you wake up to.
  • Easy date setup. You should enter your date once and never think about it again.
  • Nothing you don't need. A day counter shouldn't need your contacts, your location or a stack of permissions. Read the app's privacy details on its App Store page.
  • Fair pricing. Check what's free and what's behind a subscription before you commit.
