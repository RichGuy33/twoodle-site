Things to Doodle for Your Partner: 25 Ideas (Even If You Can't Draw)

By the Twoodle team · November 2026

Disclosure: we make Twoodle, an iPhone app for couples. The ideas below work with any drawing tool, but we've written them with a Home Screen widget in mind.

Here's the short answer: doodle something small, specific and slightly silly that only your person would understand. Not a masterpiece. A tiny dog with your partner's bedhead. A heart on top of a photo of your coffee.

Confession: our founder draws like a toddler who lost a fight with a Sharpie. That's fine. A doodle isn't a portfolio piece. It's a "hey, I thought of you" that happens to have lines in it.

Below are 25 ideas, sorted by how much effort they take (almost none, to a little). Pick one. Send it. You can be done in under a minute.

The 30-second doodles

  1. A heart on a photo. Take a picture of anything and draw one wobbly heart on it. Done.
  2. Your face as a stick figure. Add one detail: glasses, messy hair, the hat you always wear.
  3. The weather where you are. A sun, a cloud, a very sad umbrella.
  4. What you're eating. Draw the sandwich. Be dramatic about the sandwich.
  5. A tiny "hi." Just the word, in your handwriting, with an arrow pointing at nothing.
  6. A countdown. "5 sleeps" with five little moons.
  7. A thumbs-up for their big day. Interview, exam, hard meeting.

Inside-joke doodles

These are the ones that land hardest, because nobody else on earth would get them.

  1. The thing they always say. Draw it as a speech bubble coming out of a potato, or whatever fits.
  2. Your pet doing something suspicious.
  3. That one restaurant. Draw the sign. Add a tiny version of the two of you.
  4. A running gag, continued. If last week's doodle was a very smug cat, this week the cat has opinions.
  5. A "wanted" poster for their worst habit. Lovingly. Leaving wet towels on the bed, say.

Doodles for a hard day

  1. A hug, drawn badly. Two circles and a lot of arms.
  2. "Proud of you," underlined twice.
  3. A tiny blanket fort for two.
  4. A cup of tea with a face on it.
  5. A path home. A line that ends at a house with two windows lit.

Doodles for long-distance days

  1. The view from your window. Even a parking lot becomes romantic if you draw a heart over it.
  2. Two clocks. Your time and their time, with a tiny arrow between them.
  3. A plane, train or road map. The next time you'll see each other, drawn like a treasure map.
  4. A "good morning" or "good night." A sun or a moon with your initials.

Doodles to mark something

  1. An anniversary or monthsary. A cake with the wrong number of candles, on purpose.
  2. A first, remembered. Draw the place you first met. Add today's date.
  3. A plan. A rough sketch of the date you want to take them on.
  4. A thank-you. For something small they did that you noticed.

Tips if you really can't draw

  • Draw on a photo. The photo carries the picture; you add the personality.
  • Use big, simple shapes. Circles, lines, a heart. Detail makes wobbles worse.
  • Add words. A caption rescues almost any doodle.
  • Make it about them, not about art. They're not grading it.
  • Be consistent, not perfect. A small doodle every day beats one beautiful one a year.

FAQ

What should I doodle for my partner?

Something small and specific to the two of you: an inside joke, your lunch, the view from where you are. Specific beats pretty.

What if I can't draw?

It doesn't matter. A wobbly doodle made for one person reads as personal, not bad. Drawing on a photo is the easiest way to start.

Can doodles show up on my partner's Lock Screen with Twoodle?

No. Doodles appear on the Home Screen widget. Love notes can appear on the Lock Screen or Home Screen, and the Days Together widget lives on the Lock Screen.

How often should I send one?

Whatever feels natural. A small one most days is plenty, and a surprise one on a hard day lands harder than a perfect one on a quiet day.
