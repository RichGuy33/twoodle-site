Questions

Does my partner need Twoodle too?

Yes. Twoodle is built for exactly two people. You pair with one partner using an invite code, and what you send shows up in their app and widgets.

Is there an Android version?

Not right now. Twoodle is iPhone only at the moment.

Do I have to turn on location?

No. Location is only used by the optional distance widget, and only if you turn it on and allow access. Turning it off in Twoodle erases your stored position.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. Twoodle has no ads, doesn't sell personal data, and doesn't track you across other apps. Read the privacy summary or the full policy for details.
