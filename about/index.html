About Twoodle

Twoodle is a couples app made by Taking Flight, Inc. It is built around one small thing: sending your partner a doodle. On iPhone, the doodle lands on their Home Screen widget.

The iPhone app needs iOS 18 or later. Twoodle is also on Android. You can find it on the App Store and on Google Play.
